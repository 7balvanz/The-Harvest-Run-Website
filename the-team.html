Steven Balvanz

Producer and Co-Director

An executive at a Los Angeles postproduction company. He previously produced and co-directed the short documentary Martha, Meat and Potatoes about German POWs in an Iowa town during World War II. He grew up on an Iowa family farm and graduated from the University of Iowa's School of Cinema.

Aaron McAdams

Co-Director and Editor

A director and editor whose credits include Party Like the Queen of France (National Geographic Channel) and lead editing on Discovery Channel's Emmy-nominated Alaska: The Last Frontier. He also edited Woman Rebel, shortlisted for the 2010 Academy Awards, and is currently producing and editing The Selection for Film45 and History Channel. He is from Iowa and lives in Santa Monica.

Alex Wand

Composer

A Los Angeles-based, Grammy Award-winning musician and film composer who also performs as a guitarist and singer in several groups. He is from Detroit and studied composition at the University of Michigan and CalArts.

Daren Woolsey

Executive Producer

Co-Executive Producer of the Emmy Award-winning Undercover Boss, and former producer of the Emmy Award-winning Survivor. He was Executive Producer and Co-Director of the feature documentary We Will Be Brothers about the U.S. Australian Rules Football team at the 2008 International Cup. He grew up in Northern California and is based in Los Angeles.
