The Harvest Run

A Midwestern family struggles to hold onto their way of life, while facing the seemingly insurmountable hardship of economics, Mother Nature, and the changing American work ethic.

A documentary by Steven Balvanz & Aaron McAdams

The Story

The film follows the Beckley family of Colby, Kansas, who have harvested crops for three generations. Each spring they travel with their crew and equipment from Texas to the Canadian border on the "Harvest Run," a seven-month journey. The film examines their effort to keep this way of life amid economic pressure, weather, and changing attitudes toward work.
